News Navigator AI

Live  Team project

Problem

Understanding one topic usually means reading many articles. News Navigator takes a topic and returns a short briefing, so a reader can decide what is worth reading in full.

What it does

The site collects recent articles on a topic and has a language model write a summary, key points, different perspectives, possible market impact and three follow-up questions.

My part

We built it as a team. I wrote the impact model and presented the project in our demo video.

What I measured and what I only estimated

Nothing has been measured with users. My impact model assumes a reader opens 8 articles at about 3 minutes each, against about 30 seconds with the tool. That is an assumption, not a result.

Limits and next steps

The briefing is written by a language model, so it can be wrong. The next improvements are showing each article’s title, source and link beside the briefing, limiting the model to the fetched articles, clearer error messages, and automated tests.
