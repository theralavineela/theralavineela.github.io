Featured project: News Navigator AI

Team project · live baseline version

Turn a topic into a structured news briefing

Problem. Understanding one topic usually means reading many articles. News Navigator takes a topic and returns a short briefing so a reader can decide what to read in full.

My team. We built a website that combines information from multiple articles and gives brief summary points, so readers do not waste time.

How it works. A Streamlit interface sends the topic to a FastAPI /analyze endpoint. The backend fetches recent articles from a news API, then asks an LLM (Groq, Llama 3.1) for a summary, key points, differing perspectives, market impact and three follow-up questions.

Technologies. PythonFastAPIStreamlitGroq APIVercel

Impact estimate. My impact model assumes 8 articles × 3 minutes per topic versus about 30 seconds with the tool. This is an assumption-based estimate, not a measured result.

Limitations and next steps.

  • Show every article title, source and original link next to the briefing so claims can be checked.
  • Restrict the LLM to the fetched articles and label speculation.
  • Clearer error messages for missing keys and rate limits, plus automated tests.
